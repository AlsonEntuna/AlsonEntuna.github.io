Nice to meet you

Gameplay programmer turned technical artist

Nice to meet you! I'm a Gameplay Programmer turned into a Technical Artist, specializing in Tools & Pipeline, after realizing my love for tech in art.

I learned how to make pretty things programmatically and help out our team of artists achieve their maximum quality in art. I still code and learn new technologies on my spare time like game engines, graphics and how they work.

It's always been my dream to work on Tooling side of Game Engines because the joy of seeing your tools being used by people to create experiences for gamers is such an achievement for me as a programmer.
